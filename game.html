How to Play

ChumpMan is stranded in an 11-dimensional hyperspace. Your screen can only show 3 axes at once — X (left / right), Y (back / forward), Z (down / up). The other six dimensions stay hidden until you rotate them into your "3D brane" view.

Movement

Mouse

Click the screen to grab the mouse; press Esc to let go of it.

Move it left / right to walk the X axis.

Move it back / forward (up / down on the pad) to walk the Y axis.

Roll the wheel forward / backward to walk the Z axis up / down.

Keyboard

◄ ► walk the X axis left / right.

▼ ▲ walk the Y axis back / forward.

/ / ' walk the Z axis down / up.

Every other hidden dimension is stepped with the on-screen ◄ ► buttons after picking it as the active dimension with 1–9.

Touch / phone

There's no mouse-look or keyboard on a touchscreen, so every axis — X, Y, Z, Time, Attributes, all of them — moves the same way: tap its row in the Dimension Console below to make it the active dimension, then tap ◄ ► underneath the list to step it back or forward. Tapping a row's colored chip on the right reassigns that dimension to a screen axis, the touch equivalent of X/Y/Z + digit below.

Changing settings

X / Y / Z + digit

Pick a screen axis with X, Y or Z, then a digit 1–9 to send that axis to that dimension, rotating it into view so you can walk along it. (The Dimension Console does the same thing with clicks: click a lit X/Y/Z box, then an empty box on another row.) A dimension already carrying another screen axis can't take a second one — the game will let you know and refuse the pick.

T

Hands the screen's left-right input to the Time axis instead of X.

A

Hands the screen's left-right input to the Attribute axis instead of X.

S

Gives left-right input back to X, returning you to ordinary movement in your current 3D space brane.

C

Sets ChumpMan's tracker to the nearest fully-visible gold coin in your current 3D brane, and drops you out of T/A mode back into X/Y/Z movement.

V

Sets ChumpMan's tracker to the nearest vibrating string in your current 3D brane — the thing that, once you reach it, flings you to a different 3D brane view — and drops you out of T/A mode back into X/Y/Z movement.

B

Sets ChumpMan's tracker to the nearest object of any kind in your current 3D brane, and drops you out of T/A mode back into X/Y/Z movement. If nothing of the requested kind is in view for any of these three, it says so and falls back to the closest object of any kind; the sidebar's ARROWS TRACK line always shows which mode is active.

Each of these three also has its own V C B button right next to the ARROWS TRACK line — click or tap whichever one you want, no keyboard required.

Coins

There are 50 coins, each living in three dimensions. A coin glows blue (1), green (2) or gold (3) for how many of its dimensions are currently in view. Only gold coins can be collected. Gather all 50, then return to the origin — every axis at 0 — to clear the run.

Three small arrows ride along on ChumpMan, one per screen axis, pointing toward whatever's nearest — a coin, or a hidden coin's stand-in thing made real — straight along X or Z, diagonally for Y, each labeled with how many steps that way. An axis that thing doesn't need at all gets a kink instead of a straight arrow: there's no straight line for it, the rest of the distance is hidden on a dimension not currently on any screen axis. The number badge over his shoulder is separate — it always tracks distance to the nearest coin specifically.

Score

Each coin is worth 100 points when the time counter is at zero, sliding down the farther it strays from zero in either direction — losing a chunk of value for every order of magnitude away from zero (10, then 100, then 1000, positive or negative), and just as much in between as right at those marks, since it follows the counter's logarithm continuously rather than stepping down all at once. It never drops below 1 point a coin, however far the counter strays. Score is live — wind time back toward zero, from either side, and the rate climbs right back up, no matter how it got low in the first place.

T — Time

Left winds the clock backward one second, right moves it forward one — and every other kind of move, on any axis, in any mode, advances the clock a second too. Down/up (▼ ▲) and / / ' mean nothing to T (or A), so pressing one drops you straight back to ordinary X/Y/Z movement and makes that Y/Z move instead, same as pressing S first.

Winding time backward is also how you fight the score decay — but only as far as zero. A coin's value only depends on how far the counter currently sits from zero, in either direction, so winding it back toward zero brings the rate right back up no matter how high it climbed getting there; wind it past zero into negative numbers, though, and the same decay starts working against you again.

ChumpMan's position is recorded at every second he's ever passed through — along with which 3 dimensions were on X/Y/Z at that instant. Wind time back and walk forward again, and once the counter reaches a second he's already lived, its ghost — an opaque outline of exactly where he stood then — reappears on screen for that one second only, colored like a coin (blue, green, gold) by how many of its own original dimensions still happen to be on your current brane — that overlap is the only danger it poses. Land exactly on its position along every one of those overlapping dimensions and each one scores a hit: the game will warn you at one hit, warn you more sternly at two, and hits piling up to three across the run — however they arrive — ends the run.

So if you do reverse time, do your best not to meet up with yourself: stay off your past self's current line of sight — an exact match on just one dimension you still share with him — and off his current plane of sight — a match on two — since either racks up a warning. Above all, avoid being currently exactly where your past self was at that time: a match across all three dimensions you now share with him is dangerous enough by itself to end the run outright. None of this can happen, though, while you're still mid-transit on T (or steering A) — the ghost can't see you until you've dropped back into ordinary Space Mode.

Time travel can't undo a coin, though. Once collected, that location is permanently retired from coin-hood — it shows up from then on as a dashed, hollow claimed husk, in whatever form its visibility calls for, no matter how far back you wind the clock or which dimensions rotate into view. Stand exactly on one and the game will have something to say about it.

A — Attributes made real

A coin with none of its three dimensions in view isn't dropped — it stands in as a thing made real: a whale, a potted plant, Schrödinger's cat alive or dead, or aliens from the 8th dimension, good or bad, at its own fixed spot on screen. Which thing stands in for which coin is set by your position along A — move along A and they change, come back and they're the same. Rotate one of that coin's dimensions into view and the thing becomes the coin again.

The point of traveling along A is to change probability until at least one of the things you see becomes a vibrating string — once you find one, walking to it is a fast way to change your position, flinging you to a whole new 3D brane in one step rather than rotating and walking there the slow way.

Walk ChumpMan to the exact spot of one of these things — the whale, the plant, the cat alive or dead, or either alien — and it may say something, or you may just learn something about it.

Vibrating strings

A thing made real can also come up as a vibrating string. Walk ChumpMan to its exact spot and it flings him to an entirely new X / Y / Z combination — three fresh dimensions land on the screen axes (watch the Current Axis Dimensions panel change) and he lands at a fresh spot on them. The string doesn't reappear — it settles permanently into whatever it finds there: a CHUTE if you've landed on a 3D brane where no coin is completely visible, or a LADDER if you've landed on one where a coin is completely visible and ready to go collect. Stand on one later and the game will just note which it is.
